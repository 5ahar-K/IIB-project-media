Supplementary animations for Part IIB project: Optical Wireless Communication and Station-Keeping for UAVs (Cambridge Engineering, 2026). Includes animated visualisation of closed-loop beam tracking using the biased (uncalibrated) clipped-centroid estimator with a pure integrator controller over a 2-second run. The blue dot shows the true beam-centre position on the detector plane and the orange dot shows the estimated beam-centre position; the right panel tracks the cumulative RMSE over time.
The image: Closed-loop station-keeping under degraded SNR (estimator noise 10× disturbance increment). The pure integrator plateaus at 152 µm — blindly amplifying measurement noise — while proportional and Kalman both recover to ~89 µm through optimal gain selection. Analytic predictions (dotted lines) match simulated plateaus (dashed) closely, validating the closed-form expressions.
